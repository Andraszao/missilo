Designer's notes · not part of the game

One satellite, one planet, nothing explained

This build tests one thing: is tapping and bursting under gravity tense and readable with every label removed? Everything from the last version that was not needed for that is gone for now.

What is in the world

  • City lights are the health bar. Six clusters on the rim. When the last goes dark, the run ends.
  • The ring of beads round the satellite is its charge. One bead per shot.
  • Sunlight is ammunition. Beads refill in the sun and stop refilling in the planet's shadow, the darker lane on the right. Most threats come from that side.
  • Dawn is a flare from the sun and a chime. Then the next night starts, faster.
  • A satellite that gets hit goes red and dead for five seconds.

What to judge

  • Did you understand charge and shadow without reading this panel?
  • Is it fast and dangerous enough by night three?
  • Does sunlight-as-ammo feel like tension or like bookkeeping?

Deliberately missing

Items, pods, planet upgrades, more satellites, score. Step 2 adds the first pickup as an object in orbit. Step 3 is a run. The previous build with the shop is kept in the repo as prototype/v3-orbit-shop.html.
