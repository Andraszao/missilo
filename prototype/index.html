Director's notes · follows the game

    What is new here

    Games in this genre ask you to point at each threat. Missilo asks you to build a shape in the sky and let the threats fly into it. Shots persist as stars, stars link into threads, threads close into constellations, and every powerup changes what one of those three things does.

    Kept from the demo

    • Aim, fly, burst. The interceptor still detonates where you pointed.
    • 3D glowing blasts. Lit spheres with bloom, as in explosion.tscn, now with shock rings, debris and light on the town.
    • Three silos, six things to protect.
    • Chain reactions with shrinking generations.
    • Speed Boost, Blast Radius, Extra Ammo. The three .tres modifiers, one per starting silo.

    Changed

    • Shots persist. Stars, threads and constellations are the new core.
    • Powerups attach to a part: burst, star, thread or chain. A thread carries the mods of both silos that made it.
    • Resupply shows the combo before you commit. Each silo row tells you what the card will do with what is already there.
    • One input per shot. No select-then-click.
    • Four threat types, volleys with shapes.
    • A hit silo is out for the hour, not the run.

    Not in this prototype

    • Thirty or more mods, rarities, and a reroll paid for with chain salvage.
    • Named constellations. Specific shapes (a line of four, a kite) with their own effects.
    • Threats that attack the drawing: cutters that sever threads, eclipses that hide stars.
    • Longer nights with route choice, a final-hour set piece, new valleys.
    • Real art, music that layers with the size of your drawing, saves.

    What I need from you

    1. Is drawing constellations the hook? It is the part I would build the whole game around.
    2. Which combos felt good, and which did nothing for you? The mod list is the cheapest thing to change.
    3. Engine for the real build. Godot 4.5 handles this look natively: emissive meshes plus the glow you already had enabled.
    4. Phone-first or desktop-first? It decides HUD size and how silo choice works.
