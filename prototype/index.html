Designer's notes · not part of the game

Six houses hold the sky up

Try a run before reading further. The game itself says nothing, and whether it still makes sense is what this build is for.

What is in the world

  • Six houses on floating islets circle the planet. The ring of light joining them is the shield. Each house that goes dark dims it.
  • Your lantern circles further out. Its beads are shots. They refill in sunlight and not in the planet's shadow.
  • At dawn, lit houses send up balloons. Tap one to take it. More houses standing means more to choose from. What each does is for you to find out; they stack.
  • What you have taken hangs behind the lantern as a string of charms.
  • When the last house goes dark the shield breaks and something large comes out of the dark side. Break it before it lands and the sun comes up. It cracks as you hurt it.

What to judge

  • Does the last stand feel like the point of the run?
  • Could you tell what a balloon gave you after taking it?
  • Is this the right kind of painted, and what should be refined first?

Deliberately rough

The art is drawn in code as a stand-in for real gouache. Balance is untuned. Earlier builds are kept in the repo as v3-orbit-shop.html and v4-core-3d.html.
