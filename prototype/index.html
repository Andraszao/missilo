Director's notes · follows the game

    Where this sits

    Orbit, gravity-curved shots and roguelite nights with a shop between is a lane, and this prototype competes in it directly. What follows is what Missilo brings to that lane and what is still missing.

    What we bring

    • Three launchers, three orbits, three builds. Which build is over the dark side right now is a question every few seconds.
    • Aim a point, burst, chain. The demo's verb survives the move to orbit intact.
    • Gravity on both sides. Incoming hooks and slingshots; your shots inherit orbital speed and bend round the planet.
    • Items that compose. Each is pure data applied to the shot, so anything stacks with anything and synergies are found, not listed.
    • 3D glowing blasts that light the night side.

    From the demo

    • Interceptor that detonates where you pointed.
    • Three launchers, six cities, same loss rule.
    • Chain reactions with shrinking generations.
    • Speed Boost, Blast Radius, Extra Ammo as the three starting traits.
    • Emissive sphere explosions with glow.

    Not in this prototype

    • Orbit mods: raise, lower, reverse or park a satellite.
    • Moons and other bodies that bend paths and can be used as cover.
    • A hundred or more items, including ones that change orbits, add triggers (on kill, on miss, on eclipse) and transform other items.
    • Different planets per run: heavier gravity, rings, two suns.
    • Real art, music, controller support, saves.

    What I need from you

    1. Does the orbital version feel like the game? Especially: do the three orbits matter to you while playing?
    2. How much should the player fight gravity? Right now shots steer themselves to the point you tapped. A harder version makes you lead the curve yourself.
    3. How cryptic should items be? Right now each shows a flavour line and a terse effect. Isaac shows only the flavour line.
    4. Engine for the real build. Godot 4.5 handles this natively.
